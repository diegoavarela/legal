## FAQ

**How do I share a list?**
Open the list and tap Share. The other person needs an Apple account and Pendientes.

**How do I bring my tasks from another app?**
Settings → Import and export: from Reminders, by pasting a text list, or with a Pendientes file.

**Does it work on Mac?**
Yes. Pendientes runs on iPhone and Mac and syncs through your iCloud. On Mac, Quick Capture opens with ⌥ Space from any app.

¿Algo no anda o tenés una idea? Escribime a **varelad@gmail.com** y te respondo.

**¿Cómo comparto una lista?** Abrí la lista y tocá Compartir. La otra persona necesita una cuenta de Apple y Pendientes.
